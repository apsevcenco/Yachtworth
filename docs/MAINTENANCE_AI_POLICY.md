# Yachtworth Maintenance AI Policy

AI can make Maintenance stronger, but it must be constrained because maintenance data can affect safety, insurance, and vessel value.

## Allowed In V1+

- Rewrite rough crew notes into professional English.
- Extract asset metadata from manuals or invoices after user confirmation.
- Summarize service history.
- Suggest follow-up questions.
- Flag missing critical data.
- Compare entered intervals against verified templates.

## Not Allowed Without Source

- Invent manufacturer service intervals.
- Claim class or flag compliance without verified data.
- Mark a defect as safe without human confirmation.
- Create warranty or insurance conclusions without supporting documents.

## Recommended AI Flow

1. User enters or uploads source material.
2. AI extracts structured suggestions.
3. User reviews and confirms.
4. Confirmed data is saved with source metadata.
5. Audit event records the change.
